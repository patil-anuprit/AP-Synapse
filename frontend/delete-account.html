AP Synapse — Account & Data Deletion

AP Synapse users can request deletion of their AP Synapse account and data associated with that account.

How to request deletion

Step 1: Send an email from the Google account you use with AP Synapse.

Step 2: Send the request to:

ap.synapse.ai@gmail.com

Step 3: Use the subject:

AP Synapse Account Deletion Request

Include the email address associated with your AP Synapse account so the account can be identified.

Data deleted

When an eligible account deletion request is completed, AP Synapse will delete the user's AP Synapse account and account-associated data stored by AP Synapse, which may include account profile information, conversation/history data, stored preferences and uploaded content associated with the account.

Data that may be retained

Limited information may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal or regulatory obligations. Temporary backup copies, where applicable, may remain for up to 90 days before being removed through normal backup rotation.

Processing time

Account deletion requests are processed as soon as reasonably possible and normally within 30 days after the account has been verified.

Contact

For questions regarding account or data deletion, contact ap.synapse.ai@gmail.com.
