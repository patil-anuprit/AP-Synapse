AP Synapse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

AP Synapse respects user privacy and is designed to process only the information needed to provide its artificial intelligence, productivity, voice-assistant, document, and Android-assistant features.

Information We May Process

Depending on the features you choose to use, AP Synapse may process account information, prompts and conversations, uploaded files, voice or microphone input, calendar information, contacts, notification information, device-action requests, and technical diagnostics.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide AP Synapse features, generate responses, execute supported user-requested actions, maintain account functionality, improve service reliability, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot technical problems.

Android Permissions

AP Synapse may request permissions such as microphone, contacts, phone, calendar, camera, and notifications only when required for the corresponding Aprisha or AP Synapse feature. Users can revoke Android permissions at any time through their device settings.

Third-Party Services

AP Synapse may use trusted service providers for AI processing, web search, authentication, hosting, database infrastructure, email delivery, and related application services. Information may be transmitted to these providers only as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as needed to provide the service, maintain security, comply with applicable requirements, or support features requested by users. Users may request deletion of eligible account information by contacting AP Synapse.

Children

AP Synapse is not specifically designed for children under 13. Users should use the service in accordance with applicable age requirements and parental or guardian requirements where applicable.

User Control

Users control optional device permissions through Android settings and may stop using individual features at any time.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as AP Synapse develops. The current version will remain publicly available on this page.
